Fixed format — how to write your questions 
Q: What is the capital of India?
A: Mumbai
B: New Delhi
C: Chennai
D: Kolkata
Correct: B
Marks: 1
Negative: 0.25
Subject: GK
Chapter: Geography
Difficulty: Medium
Tags: capitals, india
Explanation: New Delhi has been the capital of India since 1911.

Q: 7 x 8 = ?
A: 54
B: 56
C: 58
D: 64
Correct: B

Separate each question with a blank line. Only Q, A/B/C/D and Correct are required — everything else is optional and falls back to the test's defaults. Paste as many questions as you like.
